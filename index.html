About the Conference

Shaping Tomorrow's Diplomats

IIMUN 2025 is the fifth edition of Indus International School Pune's flagship Model United Nations conference, bringing together 250+ student delegates from 30+ schools across Maharashtra and beyond.

What started in 2021 as a small intra-school event has grown into one of western India's most anticipated school MUNs. We focus on substantive debate, realistic crisis simulations, and creating a welcoming environment for first-time delegates alongside experienced MUNers.

Our seven committees cover the UN system, India's Parliament, and the media — offering something for every interest. Every delegate walks away with sharper public speaking skills, a deeper understanding of global affairs, and friendships that last beyond the conference.

Our Mission

To create an authentic diplomatic simulation where students develop critical thinking, public speaking, and negotiation skills in a supportive, academically rigorous environment.

Our Vision

A generation of Indian youth equipped to engage with global challenges — comfortable with debate, skilled in diplomacy, and confident in their ability to lead.

Our Values

Respectful debate, inclusive participation, academic integrity, and the belief that every delegate — no matter how experienced — has something valuable to contribute.
