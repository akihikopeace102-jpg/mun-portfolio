About the Conference

Shaping Tomorrow's Diplomats

The Global Diplomatic Summit (GDS) is a premier Model United Nations conference that brings together over 800 delegates from 50+ countries to engage in substantive diplomatic discourse.

Founded in 2018, GDS has grown to become one of the most respected MUN conferences in the world, known for its rigorous academic standards, innovative crisis simulations, and commitment to fostering genuine diplomatic skills among youth.

Our Mission

To cultivate the next generation of global leaders by providing an authentic simulation of international diplomacy that challenges delegates to think critically and collaborate across cultures.

Our Vision

A world where young people are equipped with the diplomatic skills, cultural understanding, and confidence needed to address the most pressing challenges facing our global community.

Our Values

Excellence in debate, integrity in diplomacy, inclusivity in participation, and innovation in crisis resolution.
